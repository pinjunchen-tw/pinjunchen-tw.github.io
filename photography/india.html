India · Photography Series

Sacred Nights and Desert Light

Architecture, ritual, daily life, and changing landscapes across Agra, Varanasi, Rajasthan, and the Thar Desert.

India revealed itself through a sequence of striking contrasts: the symmetry and stillness of the Taj Mahal, the fire and movement of the evening rituals along the Ganges, and the open horizons of the Thar Desert.

This photographic series brings together selected moments from northern India, focusing on architecture, spiritual life, landscapes, and the relationship between people and place.

Agra

Monuments and Moonlight

The Taj Mahal and the architectural heritage of Agra, seen through changing light and quiet symmetry.

Reflections in Marble

The still water mirrors the monument’s symmetry, adding another layer of balance to one of India’s most recognizable architectural landscapes.

Varanasi

Fire Along the Ganges

Evening ceremonies, riverfront life, and the changing atmosphere of the ghats in Varanasi.

Rajasthan

Horizons of the Thar Desert

Desert light, quiet movement, and the landscapes surrounding Jaisalmer in western Rajasthan.
