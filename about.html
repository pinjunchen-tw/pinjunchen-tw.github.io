About

Researcher, engineer, traveler, and lifelong learner.

Biography

I am a Ph.D. researcher in Electrical and Computer Engineering at the Georgia Institute of Technology. My research focuses on emerging memory, AI hardware, three-dimensional integration, and advanced semiconductor packaging.

I am particularly interested in connecting device-level innovations with circuit, architecture, packaging, and system-level design. My work explores how these layers can be co-optimized to improve the performance, energy efficiency, and reliability of next-generation computing systems.

Background

Before beginning my doctoral studies, I received interdisciplinary training in materials science, semiconductor technology, and electrical and computer engineering. I have also gained experience through research and industry collaborations across different institutions and technology organizations.

Beyond Research

Outside the laboratory, I enjoy photography, international travel, language learning, badminton, hiking, and exploring different cultures. This website brings together both my professional work and the experiences that shape my perspective as a researcher.
